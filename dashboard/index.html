How to read this board

Every pre-token protocol is ranked by implied value — what its token would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is. The value is risk-adjusted: a rug-risk score (0–100) built from the snapshot alone discounts it. A protocol whose TVL is fleeing, whose growth is a sudden spike, or whose record is opaque is worth less than the same TVL in a steady, old, multi-chain protocol. Nothing here predicts whether or when a token is issued, and “records no token” means the record carries none — not that none exists.

Risk signals

  • TVL flight — 7d change ≤ −25%: +35; −25 to −15%: +20; −15 to −5%: +8.
  • Pump spike — 7d change ≥ +80%: +25; +50 to +80%: +12. Mercenary inflow inflates TVL.
  • Opaque age — no listing date: +15. Young — under 90 days: +12; under 180: +6.
  • One chain — single chain with ≥ $1B TVL: +15; single chain: +8. Whale venue — ≥ $5B on ≤ 2 chains: +10.
  • No 7d data — +10. Bridge category — +10. Instant whale — over $1B TVL younger than 180 days: +20.

Only genuine launch candidates are ranked: a protocol that records no token but is a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades, an exchange's own product, a versioned pool, or in a Bridge, Liquid Staking, Liquid Restaking, Restaked BTC or CEX category is not a candidate — WBTC, Binance staked ETH and USDT0 are the textbook examples. Trust = 1 − (risk ÷ 100) × 0.6. Implied value = TVL × 0.25 × growth × maturity × trust. Growth clamps 7d change to −60…+120% at half weight; maturity rises from 0.6 to 1.0 over three years (unknown age counts as one year). Farm plans size capital down as risk rises and every plan carries an exit trigger: if 7d TVL falls more than 20% or withdrawals pause, unwind within 24 hours.
